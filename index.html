Worth knowing

The F-35B is the only F-35 variant that can land vertically. Combined lift in the hover is about 40,000 lbf.

Its internal bay stations carry 1,500 lb each, against 2,500 lb on the F-35A and F-35C.

More than 1,345 F-35s of all variants had been built by August 2026.
