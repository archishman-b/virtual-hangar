ABOUT THE PROJECT

A living aircraft study.

An editable Blender model, brought into a lightweight browser hangar. The F-35B conversion includes an opening cockpit, internal bays, coordinated gear, control surfaces and a visual lift-system demonstration.

Geometry and mechanisms are artistic approximations from public references. This is a model explorer, not a flight or engineering simulator. Stores are inert display geometry. Paint choices are fictional.

Built with Blender and Three.js. Runs locally without a subscription. Configuration saves stay in this browser.

References: Rolls-Royce LiftSystem · Martin-Baker US16E
